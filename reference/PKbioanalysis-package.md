# PKbioanalysis: Pharmacokinetic Bioanalysis Experiments Design and Exploration

Automate pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic bioanalytical procedures based
on best practices and regulatory recommendations. The package impose
regulatory constrains and sanity checking for common bioanalytical
procedures. Additionally, 'PKbioanalysis' provides a relational
infrastructure for plate management and injection sequence.
